Round 18. Concept 54 rebuilt from one parametric generator, then varied.
tools/build_variants.py writes every SVG. tools/render.js exports PNGs with
the Playwright Chromium in ~/.local/share/playwright, tools/sheet.py builds
the review sheets. 54-vector.svg is the round 17 redraw, kept for comparison.

Rebuild from this folder:
  python3 tools/build_variants.py .
  NODE_PATH=~/.local/share/playwright/node_modules node tools/render.js svg 1024 png *.svg

Changes from the round 17 redraw
- One 18 unit gap everywhere, measured as a clearance band around the bulb.
  The redraw had 12 between shade and bulb, 20 around the bulb, 22 above the stem.
- Shade has straight edges, 20 unit top corners, 12 unit bottom corners.
  The redraw's bezier top edge bowed.
- Leg is a constant 68 unit stroke with its axis through the bulb centre and
  a flat foot on the baseline. The redraw's leg tapered.
- Both scoops come from the same circle (bulb radius plus gap) with tangent
  fillets at every junction, so there are no teeth or horns.
- Bulb top and support tops share y=256. The mark is centred on the badge.

Candidates
60-soft-tips  Refined 54. Bulb radius 60, leg fillets 20, stem scoop fillet 24,
              leg toe radius 16.
57-joined-stem  Stem and shade are one piece. The gap arc flows round the
              bulb's flat end into the scoop. Strongest R at 16 and 24 px.

Explorations
55  Even gaps with bulb 56, the baseline for the rest.
56  Bulb 64, deeper scoops.
58  Full circle bulb. Reads as a lamp over a ball, weaker R.
59  Leg axis 12 units right of the bulb, spoon shaped leg top.
61  Bulb inside the shade as the R counter, plain supports. Simplest
    geometry, holds at 16 px, weaker lamp reading.
62  Candidate on the round badge at 88 percent.
63  Candidate with an ivory bulb for one colour use.
64  Bare glyph in charcoal with an orange bulb. Dark grounds need an ivory fill.

Exports: 57 and 60 at 16, 32, 64, 512 and 1024 px, RGBA with transparent corners.
