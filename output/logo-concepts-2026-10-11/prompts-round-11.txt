36-side-rocker
Use case: logo-brand. Create one minimalist app-logo exploration for Roommate. User specifically imagines a TRUE SIDE PROFILE of a human hand flicking or pressing a small physical control. This angle is essential: orthographic side elevation, as if looking along the edge of the palm, with one extended index finger and the other fingers tucked into one simple mass below it. The finger points mostly horizontally from left to right. Do NOT show the back of the hand, an open palm, a frontal pointing cursor, a hand viewed from above, or a long diagonal arm. No lamp, human head, torso, face, letters, scene or room. One compact action gesture, all foreground warm ivory, with orange negative space clearly separating contact surfaces. Bold soft geometric silhouette with minimal anatomy, broad gaps and rounded terminals; no nails, knuckles or realistic finger detail. A single rounded-square orange badge centered on a pure white square canvas, subtle warm apricot upper-left corner gradient into rich orange lower-right. Plenty of internal padding. No shadows, 3D, glossy effects, texture, motion marks, arrows, text or power glyph. The result must feel like a custom brand mark, not an instructional diagram. Direction A: fingertip pressing the raised top end of a rocker switch, all seen exactly from the SIDE. The small wall switch is at the right and represented only by a slim vertical ivory mounting edge and a short thick ivory rocker wedge leaning outward toward the approaching hand. Never show the rectangular front face of a wall plate. The side-profile hand enters horizontally from left with a short wrist, curled fingers below and a single index finger gently bending at its tip to press the protruding upper end of the rocker. Keep fingertip and rocker contact readable through a narrow orange seam. The hand is the main shape; switch is a compact small secondary shape. Large quiet silhouette, no long forearm crossing the whole badge.
